Recommended: variation 1. It is the only one where every hand-off survives and the answer follows straight on from the problem. The people's worry and their longing (58% to belong, a campus, the memories) build the need; the building then shows why the need is hard to meet on this floor; and the Loop answers both at once, landing on that same floor.

Variation 3 reaches the Loop about a minute sooner, but asks the room to accept the idea before it has felt the need, which works against the client's note that the concept must carry the weight. Variation 2 is the most logical and the least moving.
