17 · Kits of parts · acoustic strategy

Thoughtful acoustic planning enhances focus, privacy and workplace comfort. Spaces are designed with optimal NRC values (0.70–0.90) to reduce noise, minimise reverberation and improve speech privacy.

Ceiling treatment

Absorptive ceiling systems with acoustic tiles or spray treatment; dedicated ceiling treatment in meeting rooms for RT60 control.

Wall treatment

Absorptive wall panels in meeting spaces enhance speech clarity and reduce echo, with feature walls combining acoustic and aesthetic performance.

Partition design

Full-height acoustic partitions with appropriate STC ratings ensure privacy based on room function and adjacency.

HVAC noise control

Duct-borne and breakout noise controlled through HVAC design, with terminal devices meeting zone NC targets.

Sound masking

Background sound masking in the open workhalls keeps conversations private without raising voices.

Door & glazing spec

Acoustic-rated doors and double-glazing assemblies complement partition STC performance for meeting spaces.
