Why a campus · 1 · As now
Why a campus01 · Belonging

Moving offices is easy. Moving people isn’t.

Two offices. One campus.

About 640 Ackers, from two workplaces, onto one floor. A campus lets every team keep a home of its own, while everyone belongs to the same place.

Office A Office B One floor · 245 m · 91,000 sq ft 6 neighbourhoods · 1 loop

we came from two…

…we arrive on one floor…

…and live as neighbourhoods on one loop.

Like the campus where we first belonged: your own department, everyone’s pathways, courtyards and cafés.

58% of employees in India come back to the office to feel they belongCisco, “From Mandate to Magnet”, hybrid work study: 9,200 employees across Asia Pacific incl. India, Nov 2023
