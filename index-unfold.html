Unfold · 1 · Peel round
ACKO · the mark

One continuous loop.

A Möbius strip: one surface, no inside, no outside. Infinite trust, inclusivity, collaboration.

ACKO brand identity, 2025
In ACKO’s words

“It’s never us versus you;
it’s always us with you.”

A fluid shape. An endless source of power and energy.

A loop connects.
A loop surrounds.

ACKO’s own ethos, shaped into the place where ACKO works.

Many lines of business.
One loop.

like a campus: many entities, one ground

Where it bridges,
it holds.

Security and protection, built into the place.

infinite trust, made into a place
