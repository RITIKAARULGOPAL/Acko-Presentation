Design inputs.

SpaceFeedback
  1. Lean Nordic palette

    Align the color and material palette with the updated design direction (Lean nordic).

  2. Breakout spaces

    Provide more variety in breakout spaces.

  3. Collaborative space

    Ensure every neighborhood has a dedicated collaborative space.

  4. Podcast & Green room

    Increase the size of the podcast room & Green room

  5. Boardroom

    Revise the Boardroom to meet the required capacity. Location to be relooked.

  6. Meeting rooms

    Move meeting rooms toward the center of the floor plate rather than along the façade.

  7. Cafeteria

    Rework the cafeteria location, considering the unavailability of vertical pumping provisions.

  8. Landscape deck

    Explore the landscape deck as an additional functional and breakout space.

  9. Crash/nap room

    Crash/nap room: mandatory

  10. Sick room

    Sick room with a bed, privacy and first aid: mandatory

  11. Dealers room

    Dealers room to be added

  12. Mail room

    Mail room to be provided

These inputs have shaped the revised proposal.
